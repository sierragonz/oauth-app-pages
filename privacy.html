Privacy Policy

Family Calendar Sync is a personal-use script owned and operated solely by its author. It requests read-only access to the author's own Google Calendar data (via the calendar.readonly scope) for the sole purpose of displaying that data in a private, self-hosted meal-planning tool used only by the author and their household.

Calendar data fetched by this script is never shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. It is stored only in the author's own local environment and a private git repository under the author's control. No data is collected from, or made available to, any other user, because this application has no users other than its author.

This policy may be updated as the script's functionality changes. Last updated: 2026-09-17.
